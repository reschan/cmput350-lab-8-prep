To represent the same Rectangle with two triangle as desired,
Hold one triangle, e.g. (200, 150), (600, 150), (200, 450)
Make a duplicate and rotate it at the center of hypothenuse by 180 degrees.

This way we only have to hold one triangle, thus 3 points in the memory, and a rotation float. -> 16 bytes vs 24 bytes in (a)
if we use doubles instead, it'd be 20 bytes.
